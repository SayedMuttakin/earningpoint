Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

At Zenivio, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected and recorded by the Zenivio mobile application and website, and how we utilize it.

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us at support@zenivio.it.com.

This Privacy Policy complies with Google Play Store Developer Policies, including user data safety and account deletion requirements.

1. Information We Collect

Zenivio collects information to provide a better, more secure, and rewarding experience for all users. The data we collect includes:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, password, and optionally a profile picture. If you register via Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email, and public profile picture as permitted by Google's OAuth settings.
  • User-Generated Content: We store the posts, images, captions, and short-form video reels that you create and upload to the platform.
  • Chat & Communication Data: To allow real-time communication between users, our system stores and processes chat messages sent through the messenger.
  • Points and Earning Metrics: We track your engagement stats (e.g., reels watched, posts created, daily check-ins) to compute your points, leaderboard rankings, and referral rewards.
  • Device Information: We collect technical details, such as device type, operating system version, IP address, and app usage details, to optimize performance and prevent fraudulent bot activities.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, run, and maintain the Zenivio app and reward systems.
  • Personalize and expand your feed with relevant video reels and posts.
  • Process rewards, points, referrals, and verify user leaderboards.
  • Facilitate instant messaging and social connections.
  • Improve app speed, design system, and technical performance.
  • Send transactional updates, security alerts, and system notifications.
  • Maintain a safe community and prevent multi-account or bot frauds.

3. Google Play Store & AdMob Policy Compliance

We adhere closely to Google Play Store policies and integrate third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob: We partner with Google AdMob to show advertising. Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can control or reset this identifier via your mobile phone's Google Settings.
  • Data Protection: All sensitive personal data is transmitted securely via HTTPS and stored in encrypted databases.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Zenivio does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with trusted third parties necessary for app operations, including:

  • Authentication Services: Google OAuth for secure login.
  • Advertising Networks: Google AdMob for ad display.
  • Database & Hosting Services: Providers hosting our backend secure servers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We believe in giving you complete control over your data. In compliance with Google Play Store user control policies:

  • You have the right to request access, updates, or complete deletion of your data.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app by navigating to Settings > Delete Account, or by emailing us at support@zenivio.it.com. Once requested, your account and all associated data (including uploads and chat history) will be permanently erased from our servers within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Zenivio does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to remove it from our systems.
